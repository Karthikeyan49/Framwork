# Compliance, Audit and Privacy

Capture:
actor, tenant, action, resource, policy decision, metadata version, trace, time and appropriate redacted before/after evidence.

Privacy controls:
data classification, minimization, retention, masking, sandbox redaction, deletion/anonymization workflows, encrypted transport/storage, restricted telemetry.

Regulated domains get separate compliance packages and human review; do not assume generic ERP controls satisfy every regulation.

## Verification
Audit completeness, retention job, sandbox PII scan, export/delete request test where applicable.
